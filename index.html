Coverage, not availability: Dishcovery currently has dish-level research for 10 curated Manhattan restaurants. The NYC DOHMH dataset expands the restaurant master, but it does not contain menus. “Menu not indexed” means Dishcovery has not collected menu data; it does not mean a restaurant does not serve the dish.
Dish search results

Explore dishes

Dish-name matches and culinary alternatives, with menu evidence kept visible.

How recommendations work: simple searches use transparent keyword, ingredient-synonym and menu-description matching in code. Descriptive cravings are sent to an LLM that only translates them into menu terms; ranking, exclusions and dietary filters stay in code. Dish-name matches, direct ingredient overlaps and synonym-based related options are separated; a substitute is never represented as the same dish.
